02 — About

Editing since 2022

I'm Oliver Engersand, a media production student at Nannestad VGS, graduating in June 2027. I'm passionate about filmmaking and video editing, and I've been editing video since 2022.

I've done freelance editing for YouTube, made short films, and worked as a social media producer for Homesync AS. I also do multi-camera work, including on Global Gathering and the Gullklumpen film festival.
